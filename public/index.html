FAQ

Common questions.

Everything you need to know before getting started.

How much does a custom AI chatbot cost?

ChatForge offers a free tier — no API key needed. For full AI integration, API call costs are up to 10× lower than GPT-4 using Groq's LPU inference. You only pay for what you use. Custom build pricing depends on scope — ask for a quote.

How long does it take to build a custom chatbot?

Simple bots go live in 48 hours. Complex integrations with multiple REST APIs typically take under a week. We move fast — your bot won't sit in a queue for weeks.

Can the chatbot connect to my existing APIs?

Yes — that's the core feature. Your chatbot makes live HTTPS API calls to your backend in real-time: login users, fetch records, update data, run reports. Pure REST, zero mocking.

Which AI models are supported?

ChatForge supports Groq (fastest responses), Claude (best reasoning), and OpenAI GPT-4o. You can switch between models live mid-conversation — one backend, three world-class AI engines.

Do I own the chatbot code?

100% yes. You get full source code ownership. Deploy on your server, your cloud, anywhere. No monthly platform fee from us — ever.

Is the free bot really free?

Yes. The free built-in suggestion bot needs no API key and costs you nothing. It uses smart guided responses and is great for getting started. Upgrade to full AI when you're ready to scale.
